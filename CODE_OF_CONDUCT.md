# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want The Claude Factory to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on the issue at hand.
- Respect people's time: search before posting and provide the details requested.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or repeatedly bumping issues.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and issues, lock conversations, and block accounts that violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact on [@nerymurillohnd](https://github.com/nerymurillohnd)'s profile.
