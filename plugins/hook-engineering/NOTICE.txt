Derived files, all condensed and rewritten for this plugin. SKILL.md and the
references carry "Docs:" lines naming the official section each part draws on,
and references/docs-map.md lists every official heading in scope with its URL;
the agent and the report template draw on those sections through the skill:

  skills/hook-development/SKILL.md
  skills/hook-development/references/*.md
  skills/hook-development/assets/report.md
  agents/hook-engineer.md

The scripts in skills/hook-development/scripts/ are original; they implement
rules the documentation states and link to the sections they come from.
hook-changes.sh downloads the public CHANGELOG.md at run time and does not
redistribute it.
