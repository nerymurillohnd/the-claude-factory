Utilities for opting in and out of forced colors.

  - [Opting out of forced colors](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/forced-color-adjust#opting-out-of-forced-colors): `forced-color-adjust-none`
  - [Restoring forced colors](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/forced-color-adjust#restoring-forced-colors): `forced-color-adjust-auto`
  - [Responsive design](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/forced-color-adjust#responsive-design): Breakpoint variants, such as `md:`
